Celo Becomes the First Chain to Support Local Currency Agentic Payments with Ripio’s wARS, wBRL, and wCOP on Its Native x402 Facilitator Agents holding Argentine Pesos, Brazilian Reais, and Colombian Pesos can now pay without conversion, reducing FX friction on both ends of agentic commerce On July 16th, Celo Core Co. launched the native Celo x402 facilitator , strengthening one of the fastest-growing agentic ecosystems in crypto with support for stablecoin-based agentic payments. Celo’s robust stablecoin ecosystem is home to 20 currencies across 33 stablecoins, including Ripio’s wFIAT assets
