Lisk builders are invited to continue their work on Celo, a mission-aligned, technically compatible OP Stack network. Migration support is available starting today. Today, Lisk announced they will wind down the Lisk blockchain in the coming months, and invited their ecosystem builders to migrate to Celo , the Ethereum Layer 2 built for the real world with the leading programmable rails for global finance.
